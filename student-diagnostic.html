Before you begin

This test starts with 50 screening questions across 25 grammar skills. If your answers suggest a skill needs a closer look, the test automatically asks extra questions for that skill.

  • Choose one answer for every question.
  • Correct answers and explanations appear only at the end.
  • There is no timer. Work carefully and do your own work.
  • Your completed report will be saved to your school's assessment database.
