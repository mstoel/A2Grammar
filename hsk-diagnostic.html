Your Chinese progress

Each skill progresses independently. You can move to HSK 2 or HSK 3 in a skill only after passing the previous level in that same skill. A score of 75% or higher passes a level; scores from 60% to 74% trigger extra confirmation questions.

Your overall HSK level is calculated from the six core skills. HSK 2 and 3 vocabulary and character recognition are being introduced first; other skills will follow.

About this diagnostic

This is an EduAssess learning diagnostic aligned to the HSK 3.0 syllabus. It is not an official HSK examination and does not issue an HSK certificate. Writing is planned from HSK 2; speaking is not included in this first release.
