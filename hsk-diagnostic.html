HSK Chinese Diagnostic

HSK 3.0-aligned · Levels 1–3 · Skill-by-skill assessment

Chinese · HSK 1–3

Find your current Chinese level

This diagnostic is designed to identify what you can already do in Chinese and what you should practise next. Each skill is assessed separately, so your listening level may be different from your reading or grammar level.

Preview stage: The Open Tests entry is now in place. The question bank, secure scoring and student test delivery are still being built and checked. You cannot start or submit an attempt from this preview yet.

This is an EduAssess learning diagnostic aligned to the HSK 3.0 syllabus. It is not an official HSK examination and does not issue an HSK certificate.

What will be assessed?

Listening

Understand short spoken words, phrases and everyday conversations.

Picture recognition

Connect common objects, actions and situations with their Chinese meaning.

Vocabulary

Recognise and use words in familiar everyday contexts.

Character recognition

Recognise common Chinese characters and their meanings.

Reading

Understand short sentences, notices and simple texts.

Grammar

Use basic sentence patterns and grammar accurately.

How the diagnostic will work

  1. Start with HSK 1 questions in each available skill.
  2. Move to HSK 2 or HSK 3 only in skills where the previous level has been achieved.
  3. Keep passed levels and scores in your personal progress record.
  4. Receive a report showing your level and next steps for each skill.

Writing is planned to begin at HSK 2 in this diagnostic. Speaking is not included in the first release.
