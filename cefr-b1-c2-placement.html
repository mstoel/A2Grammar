CEFR B1–C2 Placement Test

Find your current English level through adaptive reading and listening. Complete two writing tasks for a fuller profile. This test is a diagnostic estimate—not an official CEFR certification.

Before you begin

ReadingAdaptive questions · 15–25 min
ListeningBrowser-generated audio · 15–25 min
Writing2 responses · 25–35 min
  • Use a quiet place and headphones for listening.
  • Do not use dictionaries, translators or AI assistance.
  • Listening recordings are generated by your browser's speech engine. Voice and pronunciation may vary by device.
  • Responses are kept in this browser session only. No account or database submission.

Writing is collected for teacher review in this prototype. It is not automatically scored, so your writing level will be shown as “Review required” rather than guessed.

Complete both tasks. Write naturally without assistance. Your writing will be included in the downloadable report for teacher review.
